These days, I keep seeing news about major tech companies cutting jobs. Amazon, Nvidia, Google, and most recently, Microsoft — they are laying off people by the thousands, and the main driving force behind it is AI.

To be honest, I’m a bit worried about myself. I just started learning Python this year and am planning to step into the financial data field. So, I did some digging around to see what's really going on.

As it turns out, with the rise of AI, the role of a Data Analyst might actually become more important. What a relief!

AI can automate and process massive amounts of data. However, we still need Data Analysts to make sense of that data and turn it into practical, actionable insights.

AI cannot replace human intuition and critical thinking. Only Data Analysts can truly understand business needs, decide which data to use, and figure out how to analyze it. Plus, the ability to communicate complex insights in a way that is easy for everyone to understand is a skill unique to Data Analysts.

So, instead of shrinking, job opportunities for Data Analysts might actually grow in the AI era. Of course, the key is becoming an analyst who knows how to leverage AI tools skillfully.
